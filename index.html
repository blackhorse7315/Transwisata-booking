Helicopter Route Management

Save preferred airport and registered VFR sequences for helicopters. Saved routes override automatic refuel routing. Admin routes can include sectors longer than 2h 15m. Daily airborne limits: Agusta 6 hours; Bell 412 7 hours. Additional sectors move to the next day when needed. A single sector cannot exceed the daily limit. The 2h 15m limit applies only to automatic refuel routing. Round trips return through the same airports and VFR points in reverse order. Fuel supply and operational approval must be confirmed separately.

From and To are included automatically when missing. Type airport codes or registered VFR names in flight order. VFR points are fly-over waypoints, not refuel stops.

Airport Fuel Prices · Pertamina & International

Store Jet A-1 / Avtur prices per liter by airport. Indonesia: upload a Pertamina price list. International: enter prices manually or import PDF / CSV / TXT. Review the extracted airport, currency and price column before saving. Include applicable supplier charges/taxes in the entered price. Import does not verify the supplier document.

For Indonesia, the three Pertamina columns above are stored together. International US cents are divided by 100 to obtain USD per liter. Domestic estimates use the chosen domestic tariff; international routes use the international tariff at Indonesian airports. The single-price field is used for foreign airports and legacy records.

Pertamina CSV columns: airport,internationalUSCentsPerLiter,domesticIDRPerLiter,domesticMultiIDRPerLiter. International CSV: airport,currency,pricePerLiter. Set the effective dates above. PDF import reads the three prices after the airport code in Pertamina order: International, Domestic, Domestic Multi. Header positions are used as a fallback. Review the populated values before saving. Scanned PDFs require OCR or manual input.
